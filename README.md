# Staff Shift Scheduler

A WPF (C#) application that automatically assigns staff to shifts based on 
availability, role, and maximum weekly hours — helping small businesses 
(e.g. hospitality) build fair, conflict-free rosters.

## Features
- Match staff to shifts based on role and availability
- Enforce maximum weekly hours per staff member
- Fair assignment (prioritises staff with fewer hours already scheduled)
- View schedule by staff member or by day
